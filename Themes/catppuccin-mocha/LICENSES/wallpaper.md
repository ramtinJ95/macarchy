# Catppuccin Mocha wallpapers

The files under `wallpapers/` are byte-for-byte copies of Omarchy's built-in
Catppuccin backgrounds at revision
`0b3f1b7ead00ac4bcbaae8bac16bab3f7efbe516`:

- <https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy/tree/0b3f1b7ead00ac4bcbaae8bac16bab3f7efbe516/themes/catppuccin/backgrounds>

Omarchy identifies the project as MIT-licensed in its root `LICENSE`. Macarchy
does not redistribute the personal Samurai override whose original artist and
license are unverified. Personal additions remain machine-local and appear as
separate labeled choices beside these package backgrounds.
